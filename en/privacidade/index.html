Privacy

NFPA framework, European legislation and GDPR. Version of 27 September 2026.

Your data are yours — always, even if you change departments

This is a design commitment. Access and, where applicable, portability rights follow GDPR arts. 15 and 20; deletion may be limited by legal obligations.

This demonstration version

Personal space supports shifts, exposure and training. By default, records stay in memory until reload or closing the page. “Save in this browser” stores them locally without additional encryption until you delete them or clear browser data. Local mode does not send them to Supabase. Online accounts are active for testing. Load and Save transfer records to the configured project; Save asks for confirmation, including exposure information. Sessions stay in memory by default. “Keep session in this tab” stores access tokens in sessionStorage for up to 12 hours, never your password. Browsers may restore tabs and sessions; sign out on shared devices. Signing out clears the screen and saved access, not local copies or drafts. Drafts are explicitly saved locally without additional encryption and can be resumed or deleted beside the form. HTML exports provide a readable report alongside technical JSON backups. You can export JSON and delete personal records. After deleting, use Save to account to update the online copy. Avoid shared devices and protect exports. Waiting-list forms do not send data until reception is configured. No advertising is used. Usage measurement is optional and starts only with consent. We collect pages and sections, active time, referring domain, device type, approximate country, site language and browser language. Form contents, health records and email are not sent to analytics. country.is receives the IP to estimate country; FireLine stores only the country code. Supabase receives events and technical connection metadata. Random browser identifiers last up to 90 days; consent preferences last 180 days. Events older than 90 days are deleted by a daily task. Refuse or withdraw consent in “Measurement preferences” to stop new measurements and remove local identifiers. Only authorised administrators can consult aggregated statistics. Basis: consent, GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and Directive 2002/58/EC. Google Fonts may receive connection metadata to serve fonts.

Waiting list

The prepared Supabase integration stores only waiting-list email and consent. No clinical data. Before enabling collection, publish the controller’s identity and contact, legal basis, recipients, retention period, withdrawal process and supervisory authority under GDPR art. 13.

Health data

The future product requires a GDPR art. 6 basis and art. 9 condition, access controls, art. 32 measures and an impact assessment where applicable (art. 35). Marketing consent does not authorise health collection. NFPA 1582/1580 medical confidentiality requirements will be assessed separately.

Retention and continuity

Directive 2004/37/EC art. 15 requires at least 40 years for carcinogen/mutagen lists and medical records after exposure ends, under national rules. This is not universal for all health information. Covered reprotoxic records require at least five years. When activities cease, records must be made available to the responsible authority as required by law. This prototype is not that archive.

Status of this information

Prototype privacy information. The legal controller and rights contact have not yet been supplied. Use fictional records in testing; real collection depends on completing these details.
