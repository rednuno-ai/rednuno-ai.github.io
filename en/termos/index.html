Terms of use

NFPA framework, European legislation and GDPR. Version of 27 September 2026.

Your data are yours — always, even if you change departments

Continuity is a product objective to be implemented through contractual and technical rules. GDPR rights and limits and Directive 2004/37/EC art. 15 archiving obligations apply.

Website scope

Demonstration website for individual firefighters and first responders. Personal space offers local records and time calculations, not clinical services, long-term archiving, operational decisions or skills certification. Continuity depends on exported copies; transfer between devices uses Load and Save to account while signed in.

Standards and legislation

NFPA 1500/1550, 1582/1580, 1851/1850 and 1001/1010 are technical references with defined scopes. Directives 89/391/EEC, 2003/88/EC and 2004/37/EC depend on national transposition and context. FireLine does not replace employer, occupational physician or commander responsibilities.

Health, compensation and qualifications

Content does not establish diagnosis, fitness, compensation rights or professional equivalence. Records can support documentary analysis without guaranteeing occupational disease recognition or compensation.

Brand and content

NFPA, European Union, NIOSH, IARC and IAFF references identify sources, not partnerships or endorsement.

Before commercial release

The provider, contacts, commercial terms and dispute-resolution options must be identified. This version does not allow purchasing a service.
