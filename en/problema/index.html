Answers to your questions.

Open only what interests you.

How is service safety organised?NFPA 1500 / 1561 → NFPA 1550

Service safety and health, and incident management. NFPA 1550:2024 consolidates 1500, 1521 and 1561. The shift module uses this safety framework without attributing European working-time limits to NFPA.

Open official source ↗
What is my exposure history for?NFPA 1582 → NFPA 1580

Occupational medical programme. NFPA 1582:2022 chapter 7 covers evaluation and medical history, including occupational exposure. NFPA 1580:2025 consolidates health and wellness standards. Assessment belongs to a qualified professional.

Open official source ↗
Why record PPE cleaning?NFPA 1851 → NFPA 1850

PPE selection, care and maintenance. NFPA 1851:2020 section 4.3 guides records for each item, including identification and cleaning history. NFPA 1850:2025 consolidates 1851 and 1852. The example is partial and does not certify full compliance.

Open official source ↗
Does recording training give me certification?NFPA 1001 → NFPA 1010

Professional qualifications. NFPA 1010:2024 consolidates standards including NFPA 1001. It helps organise skills, without automatic recognition of training in Portugal or another country.

Open official source ↗
What rules apply to rest?Directive 2003/88/EC

Working time: 11 consecutive hours of rest per 24 hours (art. 3) and an average maximum 48-hour week including overtime (art. 6). Application depends on scope, reference periods, derogations and national transposition.

Open official source ↗
Why keep records for 40 years?Directive 2004/37/EC

Protection against carcinogens, mutagens and reprotoxic substances. Arts. 3 and 12: assessment and exposed worker lists. Arts. 14–15: surveillance and archiving. At least 40 years after exposure ends for carcinogens/mutagens; at least five for reprotoxic substances. Legal scope and national rules apply.

Open official source ↗
What protection do I have at work?Directive 89/391/EEC

General occupational safety and health: prevention, risk assessment, information and training (arts. 6, 9, 10 and 12). Applicability considers specific rules and exceptional situations under art. 2.

Open official source ↗
Who may process my health data?Regulation (EU) 2016/679 · GDPR

Health data are a special category (art. 9). Processing requires a basis under art. 6 and a condition under art. 9, plus security and transparency. GDPR itself does not require 40-year retention; that comes from the applicable occupational regime.
