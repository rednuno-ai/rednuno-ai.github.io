NFPA 1582: history supporting health

Editorial · 27 September 2026 · Official references in the article

NFPA 1582:2022 covers firefighters’ occupational medical programmes. Chapter 7 addresses member evaluations, including medical and occupational history. Exposure records provide information, not a medical examination.

From NFPA 1582 to NFPA 1580

NFPA 1580:2025 consolidates health and wellness standards, including NFPA 1582. Confirm the adopted edition and requirement mapping. Directive 2004/37/EC, art. 14 and Annex II, covers health surveillance for activities within its scope.

The FireLine example is partial, documentary and non-clinical. It is not an official NFPA questionnaire or a fitness assessment.
