NFPA 1851: PPE also needs a history

Editorial · 27 September 2026 · Official references in the article

NFPA 1851:2020, section 4.3, covers records per equipment element, including identification and care. Dates, results and cleaning personnel make the history traceable.

Maintain equipment, document care

NFPA 1850:2025 consolidates 1851 and 1852. Confirm the adopted edition. In the EU, Directive 89/391/EEC covers prevention and Directive 2004/37/EC protective measures for covered activities.

Cleaning records are only part of the PPE programme. A completed screen does not certify decontamination effectiveness or full standard compliance.
