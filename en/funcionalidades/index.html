How is my data handled?

Local mode does not send records to a server. When the account is enabled, Save to account sends records to Supabase after confirmation. Browser storage has no additional encryption: avoid shared devices. Keeping a session uses this tab’s storage for up to 12 hours; signing out removes saved access. Drafts stay locally until deleted or added to history. Exports may contain exposure information; protect them. This is not a clinical archive and does not guarantee statutory 40-year retention. GDPR, arts. 9 and 32; EU 2004/37/EC, art. 15; NFPA 1582/1580. More details.
